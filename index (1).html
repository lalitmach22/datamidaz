What we build

Four things, done properly: business and data analytics that hold up to scrutiny, dashboards people trust, AI woven into how decisions actually get made, and automation that quietly removes the busywork.

Business & data analytics

Before we build anything, we do the analysis properly — cleaning and modeling your data, finding out what's actually driving revenue, cost, or churn, and framing it in business terms your leadership team can act on, not just a data team can read.

Custom dashboards

We design dashboards around the decisions you need to make, not the tables your data happens to sit in. Every view is built for the person who has to act on it — a sales lead, a plant manager, a CFO — so nobody has to dig for the number that matters.

How we work

A small, senior team, working close to your business rather than at a distance from it. Four stages, no black boxes.

1. Listen

Understand the decision

We start with the calls your team actually needs to make, and where today's data makes that harder than it should be.

2. Design

Design the system

We map the data, define what "good" looks like, and design dashboards or models around real decisions, not vanity metrics.

3. Build

Build and integrate

We build on top of the tools you already use, connecting sources cleanly instead of asking you to rip and replace.

4. Decide

Hand over and support

We train your team, stay close after launch, and keep refining as your business and your data change.
